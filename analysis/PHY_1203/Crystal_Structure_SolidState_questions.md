## Part A — Actual Previous-Year Questions (Verbatim / Near-Verbatim from 2020–2023)

### 2020 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q6(a) — [3 marks]**
What is packing fraction? Why the packing fraction value is less than 1? Mention which structure has large value of packing fraction.

**Q6(b) — [7 marks]**
What is a Miller index? Show that for cubic crystal structure the interplanar distance $d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}$.

**Q6(c) — [? marks] (≈4)**
The lattice constant of a simple lattice is $a$. Find the lattice spacing between (111), (112) and (113) lattice planes.

### 2021 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q5(a) — [5 marks]**
Define packing fraction. Calculate the packing fraction for Simple cubic crystal structure.

**Q5(b) — [6 marks]**
What do you mean by the term Miller indices? Show that the inter-planar distance of orthorhombic crystal is given by $d = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\frac{h^2}{a^2}+\frac{k^2}{b^2}+\frac{l^2}{c^2}}}$

**Q5(c) — [3 marks]**
Show that in a simple cubic lattice the separation between the successive lattice planes (100), (110) and (111) are in the ratio $1:0.71:0.58$.

### 2022 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q4(a) — [3 marks]**
Define crystal lattice, unit cell, and coordination number.

**Q4(b) — [7 marks]**
Show that for cubic crystal structure the interplanar distance, $d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}$.

**Q4(c) — [4 marks]**
Sketch the following planes of a cubic unit cell: (011), ($0\bar{1}0$), (110), ($\bar{1}11$)

**Q5(a) — [3 marks]**
Define: (i) Bravais Lattice (ii) Primitive Cell (iii) Miller index

**Q5(b) — [7 marks]**
Show that for bcc and fcc crystal structures the lattice constants are given by $a_{bcc} = \frac{4r}{\sqrt{3}}$ and $a_{fcc} = \frac{4r}{\sqrt{2}}$.

**Q5(c) — [4 marks]**
In a crystal, a lattice plane cuts intercepts of $a$, $2b$, $3c$ along the three axes, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are primitive vectors of the unit cell. Determine the Miller indices of the given plane.

### 2023 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q5(a) — [6 marks] ($3+3$)**
Explain the Band Theory of Solids and illustrate it with a diagram? What are the main differences between n-type and p-type semiconductors?

**Q5(b) — [5 marks]**
How can you determine the inter-planar spacing in a crystal lattice given the wavelength of incident X-rays and the diffraction angle? Derive the equation for this.

**Q5(c) — [3 marks]**
Sodium crystallizes in a body-centered cubic (BCC) structure. Given its density of $9.6 \times 10^2\,\text{kg/m}^3$ and atomic weight of 23, calculate the lattice constant of sodium.

**Q6(a) — [4 marks]**
Define Miller indices. Also write down some important features of Miller indices.

**Q6(b) — [7 marks] ($2+5$)**
What do you mean by diffraction of X-rays by crystal planes? Derive Bragg's law of diffraction.

**Q6(c) — [3 marks — placed under solid-state in paper]**
A source of sound has a frequency of 512 hz and an amplitude of 0.25 cm. What is the flow of energy across a square cm per second, if the velocity of sound in air is 340 m/s and the density of air is $0.00129\,\text{g/cm}^3$?

> **Interlink:** 2023 Q5(b)/Q6(b) are Bragg's law derivations; 2022 Q5(b) bcc/fcc lattice constants and 2023 Q5(c) Na BCC lattice constant form a numerical pair.

## Part B — 10 Original Practice Questions (Exam-Realistic)

> Same style, marks and format as PHY-1203 finals. Enriches concepts not merely rephrased.

### **[Easy]**

**P1. [3 marks] [Easy]**
Define (i) crystal lattice (ii) basis (iii) unit cell. Distinguish between primitive and non-primitive (conventional) cell with one example each (sc primitive, fcc conventional).

**P2. [3 marks] [Easy]**
Define packing fraction (atomic packing factor). Why is it always $<1$? Arrange SC, BCC, FCC in increasing packing fraction and give their ideal values ($0.52$, $0.68$, $0.74$).

**P3. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Define Miller indices. Write the rules to determine $(hkl)$ from intercepts: reciprocals → clear fractions. What are features: (i) parallel planes same indices (ii) intercepts at infinity → 0 (iii) negative → bar.

### **[Medium]**

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Derive $d_{hkl}= a/\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}$ for cubic using equation of plane $hx+ky+lz=a$ and distance from origin. Then calculate $d_{100}$, $d_{110}$, $d_{111}$ for $a=4\,\text{Å}$ and verify ratio $1:0.71:0.58$.

**P5. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Derive Bragg's law $2d\sin\theta = n\lambda$ with diagram (path difference between two adjacent planes). An X-ray of $\lambda=1.54\,\text{Å}$ (Cu $K_{\alpha}$) diffracts from (111) planes of FCC with $a=3.6\,\text{Å}$ at what $\theta$ for first order?

**P6. [5 marks] [Medium]**
A BCC metal has atomic radius $1.24\,\text{Å}$. Estimate its lattice constant $a_{bcc}=4r/\sqrt3$ and density if atomic weight $55.8$ (Fe) with 2 atoms/cell: $\rho = 2M/(N_A a^3)$. Compare with FCC of same radius.

### **[Hard]**

**P7. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Orthorhombic crystal has $a=3$Å, $b=4$Å, $c=5$Å. Derive general $d_{hkl}=1/\sqrt{(h/a)^2+(k/b)^2+(l/c)^2}$ and calculate $d_{111}$ and $d_{123}$. Explain reduction to cubic when $a=b=c$.

**P8. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Explain Band Theory of solids: formation from atomic levels, valence band, conduction band, forbidden gap. Distinguish conductor, semiconductor, insulator via band gap. Differentiate n-type (donor, electrons majority, Fermi near $C_B$) vs p-type (acceptor, holes majority, Fermi near $V_B$) with diagrams and doping examples (P in Si vs B in Si).

**P9. [4 marks] [Hard]**
A plane cuts intercepts $2a$, $3b$, $1c$ (or $a, 2b, 3c$ as in 2022 Q5c) along axes. Determine Miller indices. Sketch (011) and (111) planes in a cubic cell and indicate the origin shift if plane passes through origin.

**P10. [5 marks] [Hard]**
Sodium BCC: density $\rho=0.97\,\text{g/cm}^3$, atomic weight $23$, $N_A=6.02×10^{23}$. Use $\rho = nM/(N_A a^3)$ with $n=2$ to calculate $a$. If X-ray of $\lambda=0.071\,\text{nm}$ shows first-order reflection from (110) at $\theta=15^{\circ}$, estimate $d_{110}$ and verify consistency with $a$.
