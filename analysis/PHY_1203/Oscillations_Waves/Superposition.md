## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P6. [6 marks] [Medium]**
Two SHMs of same $\omega$ act at right angles: $x= a\sin\omega t$, $y= b\sin(\omega t + \phi)$. Describe the Lissajous figure for $\phi = 0, \pi/2, \pi$. Then for collinear superposition $y_1=3\sin\omega t$, $y_2=4\sin(\omega t+90^{\circ})$, find resultant amplitude and phase.

**P-New-I1. [4 marks] [Easy] — New**
Two collinear SHMs $y_1 = 2\sin(\omega t+\pi/6)$ and $y_2 = 2\sin(\omega t+\pi/3)$ superpose. Use phasor $A^2=A_1^2+A_2^2+2A_1A_2\cos(\phi_2-\phi_1)$ and $\tan\delta = (A_1\sin\phi_1+A_2\sin\phi_2)/(A_1\cos\phi_1+A_2\cos\phi_2)$ to find $A$ and $\delta$. Verify with complex exponential method.

**P-New-I2. [5 marks] [Medium] — New**
Superposition of $y_1=5\sin(100\pi t)$ and $y_2=5\sin(104\pi t)$ gives beats. Derive $y = 2A\cos(\Delta\omega t/2)\sin(\bar{\omega}t)$ and find beat frequency. Sketch envelope and explain why beat frequency is $|\nu_1-\nu_2|$.
