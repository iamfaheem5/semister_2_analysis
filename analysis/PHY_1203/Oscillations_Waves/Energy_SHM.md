## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium] — energy part**
A 0.5 kg block attached to a spring ($k=200\,\text{N/m}$) oscillates with amplitude 10 cm. Calculate total mechanical energy, maximum speed and maximum acceleration. At what displacement is $KE = PE$?
> *Energy calculation is the focus here; kinematic part also → [Spring_Mass_Numericals.md](./Spring_Mass_Numericals.md).*

**P-New-F1. [4 marks] [Easy] — New**
Write $KE = \frac12 m\dot{x}^2 = \frac12 m\omega^2A^2\sin^2(\omega t+\delta)$ and $PE = \frac12 kx^2 = \frac12 kA^2\cos^2(\omega t+\delta)$ for SHM. Prove $E=KE+PE = \frac12 kA^2$ constant and $\langle KE\rangle = \langle PE\rangle = E/2$ over one period (use $\langle \sin^2\rangle=\langle\cos^2\rangle=1/2$).

**P-New-F2. [4 marks] [Medium] — New**
A particle in SHM has total energy $E=0.02\,\text{J}$ with $m=0.1\,\text{kg}$, $A=0.05\,\text{m}$. Find $\omega$, $k$, and the displacement where $KE = 2\,PE$. Sketch $KE$, $PE$, $E$ vs $x$ and mark crossing points.
