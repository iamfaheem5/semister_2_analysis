## Part A — Actual Previous-Year Questions (Verbatim / Near-Verbatim from 2020–2023)

### 2020 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q3(a) — [3 marks]**
Define periodic motion and damped vibrations.

**Q3(b) — [8 marks]**
Show that the total energy of the system is the same as the maximum value of any one of the two forms of energy.

**Q3(c) — [3 marks]**
A spring hung vertically, is found to be stretched by 0.02m from its equilibrium position when a force of 4N acts on it. Then a 2 kg body is attached to the end of the spring and is pulled 0.04m from its equilibrium positions along the vertical line. What is the mechanical energy of the oscillating system?

**Q4(a) — [3 marks]**
What Simple Harmonic Oscillation? Write down its characteristics.

**Q4(b) — [7 marks]**
Show that the average kinetic energy and average potential energy is same and constant and half of total energy.

**Q4(c) — [4 marks]**
A body describing SHM has a maximum acceleration of $8\pi\,\text{m/s}^2$ and a maximum speed of $1.6\,\text{m/s}$. Find the time period $T$ and the amplitude $a$.

### 2021 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q3(a) — [3 marks]**
What Simple Harmonic Oscillation? Write down its characteristics.

**Q3(b) — [7 marks]**
Show that maximum K.E and maximum potential energy is same and equal to total energy.

**Q3(c) — [4 marks]**
A body is vibrating with simple harmonic motion of amplitude 15 cm and frequency 4 Hz. Compute (i) the maximum acceleration and velocity (ii) the acceleration and velocity when the displacement is 9 cm.

**Q4(a) — [3 marks]**
What is (i) Particle velocity (ii) damped harmonic oscillation (iii) reduced mass

**Q4(b) — [7 marks]**
For damped harmonic oscillation prove that, $x = e^{-bt} A \cos(wt + \delta)$

**Q4(c) — [4 marks]**
The velocity of a simple harmonic wave is $30\,\text{cm/s}$. At a time $t=0$ the displacement of a particle is given by $y = 4 \sin 2\pi \frac{x}{100}$. Find the equation for the displacement at a time $t=2\,\text{s}$.

### 2022 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q3(a) — [3 marks]**
Define simple harmonic motion and explain its characteristics.

**Q3(b) — [7 marks]**
Derive an expression for the differential equation of motion of a body executing simple harmonic motion and find out the solution of it.

**Q3(c) — [4 marks]**
Two simple harmonic motions acting simultaneously on a particle are given by the equations $y_1 = 2 \sin(\omega t + \frac{\pi}{6})$ and $y_2 = 3 \sin(\omega t + \frac{\pi}{3})$. Calculate (i) amplitude and (ii) phase constant of the resultant vibration.

**Q6(a) — [4 marks]**
Explain the terms 'Phase velocity' and 'Group velocity'.

**Q6(b) — [6 marks]**
Obtain an expression for the differential equation of a plane progressive wave.

**Q6(c) — [4 marks]**
Verify that the harmonic wave function $\Psi = A \sin(kx - \omega t)$ is a solution of the one-dimensional differential wave equation.

### 2023 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q6(c) — [3 marks]**
A source of sound has a frequency of 512 hz and an amplitude of 0.25 cm. What is the flow of energy across a square cm per second, if the velocity of sound in air is 340 m/s and the density of air is $0.00129\,\text{g/cm}^3$?

**Q7(a) — [6 marks]**
Prove that the differential equation governing two-body oscillations is identical to the differential equation of simple harmonic motion (SHM) for a single oscillating mass.

**Q7(c) — [3 marks]**
A body describing SHM has a maximum acceleration of $8\pi\,\text{m/s}^2$ and a maximum speed of $1.6\,\text{m/s}$. Find the period $T$ and amplitude $A$.

## Part B — 10 Original Practice Questions (Exam-Realistic)

> Same style, marks and format as PHY-1203 finals. Enriches concepts not merely rephrased.

### **[Easy]**

**P1. [3 marks] [Easy]**
Define SHM and write its differential equation $\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} + \omega^2 x = 0$. List any four characteristics (isochronism, sinusoidal, restoring force $\propto -x$, phase).

**P2. [3 marks] [Easy]**
Distinguish between periodic motion, simple harmonic motion and damped vibration with one example each. What is reduced mass $\mu = m_1 m_2/(m_1+m_2)$ and where is it used?

**P3. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Define particle velocity and wave velocity. A transverse wave has $y = 5 \sin(2\pi t - 0.02\pi x)$ (cm, s). Find (i) amplitude (ii) frequency (iii) wavelength (iv) wave speed and identify phase velocity.

### **[Medium]**

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium]**
A 0.5 kg block attached to a spring ($k=200\,\text{N/m}$) oscillates with amplitude 10 cm. Calculate total mechanical energy, maximum speed and maximum acceleration. At what displacement is $KE = PE$?

**P5. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Show that $x(t)=A\cos(\omega t + \delta)$ satisfies $\ddot{x}+\omega_0^2 x =0$. A particle in SHM has $x=0$ at $t=0$ moving positive with $v_{max}=0.2\,\text{m/s}$ and $T=2\,\text{s}$. Write its displacement equation and find $x$ at $t=0.5\,\text{s}$.

**P6. [6 marks] [Medium]**
Two SHMs of same $\omega$ act at right angles: $x= a\sin\omega t$, $y= b\sin(\omega t + \phi)$. Describe the Lissajous figure for $\phi = 0, \pi/2, \pi$. Then for collinear superposition $y_1=3\sin\omega t$, $y_2=4\sin(\omega t+90^{\circ})$, find resultant amplitude and phase.

### **[Hard]**

**P7. [6 marks] [Hard]**
For damped oscillator $m\ddot{x}+b\dot{x}+kx=0$, assume $x=e^{\lambda t}$, derive characteristic equation and show subcritical solution $x = Ae^{-bt/2m}\cos(\omega' t + \delta)$ where $\omega' = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - (b/2m)^2}$. Discuss critical and overdamped cases. What is $Q$-factor?

**P8. [5 marks] [Hard]**
Derive the one-dimensional wave equation $\frac{\partial^2 y}{\partial t^2} = v^2 \frac{\partial^2 y}{\partial x^2}$ for a stretched string using transverse force on small element. Verify that $y = A\sin(kx-\omega t)$ satisfies it iff $v=\omega/k$, and deduce $v = \sqrt{T/\mu}$.

**P9. [6 marks] [Hard]**
The equation of a plane progressive wave is $y = 8 \sin 2\pi\left(\frac{t}{0.02} - \frac{x}{50}\right)$ cm. Find (i) amplitude, wavelength, frequency, wave speed (ii) particle velocity $v_p = \partial y/\partial t$ and particle acceleration at $x=25\,\text{cm}, t=0.01\,\text{s}$ (iii) phase difference between two particles 10 cm apart.

**P10. [4 marks] [Hard]**
Define phase velocity $v_p=\omega/k$ and group velocity $v_g = d\omega/dk$. For a dispersive medium $\omega = ck(1 + \alpha k^2)$, show $v_g \neq v_p$. A wave packet with central $k_0$ travels: explain physically why energy travels with $v_g$, not $v_p$.
