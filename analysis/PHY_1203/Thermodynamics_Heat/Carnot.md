## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P6. [6 marks] [Medium]**
A Carnot engine operates between $400\,\text{K}$ and $300\,\text{K}$ absorbing 600 J per cycle from the hot reservoir. Calculate (i) efficiency (ii) heat rejected (iii) work per cycle. If it is run in reverse as a refrigerator, what is its COP?

**P-New-C1. [4 marks] [Easy] — New**
Draw the $P$-$V$ and $T$-$S$ diagrams for a Carnot cycle and label the four stages (isothermal expansion, adiabatic expansion, isothermal compression, adiabatic compression). State where $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ are exchanged.

**P-New-C2. [5 marks] [Hard] — New**
A Carnot refrigerator operates between $0^{\circ}\text{C}$ and $27^{\circ}\text{C}$ with COP $= T_2/(T_1-T_2)$. If 500 J of work is supplied per cycle, how much heat is extracted from the cold reservoir and how much is rejected to the hot reservoir? Show that $\eta_{engine} = 1/(COP_{ref}+1)$.
