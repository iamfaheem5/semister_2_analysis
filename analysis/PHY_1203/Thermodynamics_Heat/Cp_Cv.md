## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Derive $C_p - C_v = R$ for one mole of ideal gas using first law and $PV=RT$. Explain where degrees of freedom $f$ enters to give $C_v = \frac{f}{2}R$ and $\gamma = 1+2/f$. Evaluate $\gamma$ for monoatomic and diatomic gases.

**P-New-D1. [4 marks] [Easy] — New**
Define degrees of freedom $f$. How many $f$ does a monoatomic, diatomic (rigid and with vibration), and polyatomic gas have? Use equipartition $ \langle E \rangle = \frac12 kT$ per quadratic term to find $C_v$ and $\gamma$ for each.

**P-New-D2. [4 marks] [Medium] — New**
A diatomic gas at $300\,\text{K}$ has $C_v = 20.8\,\text{J mol}^{-1}\text{K}^{-1}$. Infer $f$ and $\gamma$, then compute its mean molecular kinetic energy per mole and per molecule ($R=8.314$, $N_A=6.02\times10^{23}$). Compare with 2022 Q2(c).
