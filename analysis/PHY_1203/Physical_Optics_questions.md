## Part A — Actual Previous-Year Questions (Verbatim / Near-Verbatim from 2020–2023)

### 2020 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q5(a) — [3 marks]**
Write short notes on (i) interferometer (ii) resolving power

**Q5(b) — [7 marks]**
Show that interference obeys the conservation principle of light.

**Q5(c) — [4 marks]**
Green light of wavelength $5100\,A^{\circ}$ from a narrow slit is incident on a double slit. If the overall separation of 10 fringes on a screen, $200\,\text{cm}$, away is $2\,\text{cm}$, find the slit separation.

**Q7(a) — [3 marks]**
Define (i) Newton's ring (ii) Coherent source (iii) Retardation plates

**Q7(b) — [7 marks]**
Describe the Brewster's Law. From Brewster's law show that the polarizing angle of incidence, the reflected and refracted rays are $90^{\circ}$ apart.

**Q7(c) — [4 marks]**
Light from a sodium vapour lamp ($\lambda=0.58\,\mu\text{m}$) forms an interference pattern on a screen $1.00\,\text{m}$ from a pair of slits. The bright fringes in pattern are $30\,\text{mm}$ apart. What is the slit separation?

### 2021 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q6(a) — [5 marks]**
What are Newton's rings and discuss why the Centre of Newton's rings is always dark.

**Q6(b) — [5 marks]**
In the phenomena of interference, show that the width of the bright and the dark fringe are always equal.

**Q6(c) — [4 marks]**
In a Newton's ring experiment, the diameter of $15^{\text{th}}$ ring was found to be $0.59\,\text{cm}$ and that of the $5^{\text{th}}$ ring was $0.336\,\text{cm}$. If the radius of the plano-convex lens is $100\,\text{cm}$, calculate the wavelength of light.

**Q7(a) — [3 marks]**
Define the terms: Polarization of light, Double refraction and Nicol prism.

**Q7(b) — [7 marks]**
State and explain the Brewster's Law. From Brewster's law show that the polarizing angle of incidence, the reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular to each other.

**Q7(c) — [4 marks]**
The refractive index of plastic is 1.25. Calculate the angle of refraction for a ray of light incident at polarizing angle.

### 2022 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q7(a) — [3 marks]** *(definition portion)*
Define any three: (i) Equipartition of energy (ii) Entropy (iii) Coherent source (iv) damped harmonic oscillation (v) Huygen's principle (vi) double refraction.

**Q7(b) — [7 marks]**
What is Brewster's law? From Brewster's law show that the reflected and refracted rays are perpendicular to each other.

### 2023 — PHY-1203 Year Final

**Q2(a) — [4 marks]**
What are the differences between polarized light and unpolarized light?

**Q2(b) — [6 marks]**
From Newton's rings prove that the radius of the $m^{\text{th}}$ dark ring is proportional to $\sqrt{\lambda}$ (under root of wavelength).

**Q2(c) — [4 marks]**
Define refractive index. In the experiment of Newton's rings, diameter of the eighth ring is $0.4\,\text{cm}$ and of the third ring is $0.2\,\text{cm}$. Radius of curvature of the lens is $101\,\text{cm}$. Calculate the wavelength of the light used.

**Q4(a) — [5 marks]**
Prove that the expression for the fringe width in a double-slit experiment is $\frac{\lambda D}{a}$.

**Q4(b) — [6 marks] ($2+4$)**
What is a Fresnel Biprism, and how does it work? What are the key differences between Fresnel diffraction and Fraunhofer diffraction?

**Q4(c) — [3 marks]**
Light from a sodium vapor lamp ($\lambda=589\,\text{nm}$) forms an interference pattern on a screen $0.8\,\text{m}$ from a pair of slits. The bright fringes in the pattern are $0.35\,\text{cm}$ apart. What is the slit separation?

> **Note:** 2020 Q5(c) & 2020 Q7(c) are classic Young's double-slit fringe-width problems; 2021 Q6(c) & 2023 Q2(c) are Newton's-rings $\lambda$ determinations — both repeat with different numbers.

## Part B — 10 Original Practice Questions (Exam-Realistic)

> Same style, marks and format as PHY-1203 finals. Enriches concepts not merely rephrased.

### **[Easy]**

**P1. [3 marks] [Easy]**
Define (i) coherent sources (ii) interference (iii) fringe width $\beta$. State the two conditions for sustained interference (coherence + path difference).

**P2. [3 marks] [Easy]**
What is Brewster's law? Write $\tan\theta_p = \mu$ (also $n = \tan p$). If $\mu=1.52$ for glass, calculate the polarizing angle. What happens to the reflected ray at this angle?

**P3. [4 marks] [Easy]**
State Huygens' principle for wave propagation. Use it to explain (with diagram) why a plane wavefront after passing a slit spreads (diffraction) and why Snell's law follows from Huygens construction (brief).

### **[Medium]**

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Young's double-slit: Derive fringe width $\beta = \lambda D / d$ (where $d=a$ is slit separation) for bright fringes using $d\sin\theta = m\lambda$ and small-angle geometry. Show dark fringe width is same. A double slit with $d=0.2\,\text{mm}$ illuminated by $600\,\text{nm}$ gives $\beta$ at $D=1.5\,\text{m}$?

**P5. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Newton's rings: Derive condition for dark ring radius $r_m = \sqrt{m\lambda R}$ (and bright $r_m = \sqrt{(m+1/2)\lambda R}$) using air film $2t = m\lambda$ with $t = r^2/2R$. Explain why centre is dark (extra $\lambda/2$ phase on reflection). Calculate $r_{10}$ for $\lambda=589\,\text{nm}$, $R=1\,\text{m}$.

**P6. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Explain Fresnel Biprism: construction (two prisms base-to-base) and how it creates virtual coherent sources. Mention use to determine $\lambda$. Differentiate Fresnel vs Fraunhofer diffraction in a table: wavefront, distance, lens needed, example (biprism/Fresnel vs grating/Far-field).

### **[Hard]**

**P7. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Prove conservation of energy in interference: Intensities $I_1,I_2$, resultant $I = I_1+I_2+2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos\phi$. Show average over one fringe system $\langle I\rangle = I_1+I_2$ (energy redistributed, not destroyed). Discuss why dark fringe is not zero if $I_1\neq I_2$.

**P8. [5 marks] [Hard]**
A Fresnel biprism experiment uses slit to biprism $40\,\text{cm}$, biprism to eyepiece $60\,\text{cm}$, distance between virtual sources $0.4\,\text{mm}$, fringe width $0.3\,\text{mm}$ measured. Calculate wavelength. Then if sodium $589\,\text{nm}$ with same geometry what is new fringe width?

**P9. [4 marks] [Hard]**
Unpolarized light passes through a polarizer and then a Nicol prism (or Polaroid) at $60^{\circ}$ to first polarizer. Using Malus' law $I = I_0\cos^2\theta$, compute transmitted intensity fraction ($0.5 \times \cos^2 60^{\circ}$). Explain double refraction in calcite: $o$-ray and $e$-ray, and action of Nicol prism to eliminate $o$-ray by total internal reflection.

**P10. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Define retardation plates: quarter-wave ($\lambda/4$) and half-wave ($\lambda/2$) for calcite/quartz $t = \lambda/4(\mu_o-\mu_e)$. A $600\,\text{nm}$ wave passes through a quarter-wave plate of calcite ($\mu_o=1.658$, $\mu_e=1.486$). Calculate minimum thickness required. Explain how a quarter-wave plate converts plane-polarized to circularly polarized when incident at $45^{\circ}$ to optic axis.
