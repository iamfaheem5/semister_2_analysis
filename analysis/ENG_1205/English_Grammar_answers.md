# English Grammar — Answers

## Part A — Model Answers (Condensed)

### 2020 Q4 Corrections
(a) I'm not in the habit **of taking** heavy **lunch**. (habit of + gerund)
(b) It was the first time they **had had**. / It was the first time they had had it.
(c) I look forward **to receiving** your reply. (look forward to + gerund)
(d) I can't **but laugh** at this. / I cannot help laughing. (can't but + bare infinitive)
(e) The **cost of** daily necessaries **is** very high. / Daily necessaries are costly.
(f) I feel **like crying**. (feel like + gerund)
(g) A man is known **by the company he keeps**. (keep → keeps, no is kept)

### 2020 Q5 Verbs
(a) are (b) is born (c) know (d) start (e) thinking (f) nursing (g) bringing (h) have remained / remain (i) taking (j) should offend / offend.
Full: Parents **are**, child **is born**, joys **know**, They **start thinking**, of **nursing** and **bringing**, They **have remained** safe, mind **taking**, We never **offend**.

### 2020 Q6 Articles
(a) the (b) A (c) the (d) a (e) the (f) a (g) the (h) X / the? (importance of → the) (i) the (j) X / the? Typically: (a) the key, (b) A person, (c) the rules, (d) a long way, (e) the world, (f) a man, (g) the importance, (h) X sincerity? But (h) sincerity uncountable → X, (i) the benefit, (j) X success → × success? Often "× success". Variation accepted if justified.

### 2020 Q7 Prepositions
(a) in (b) on (c) in (d) after (e) On (f) with (g) in/with (h) in (i) in (j) of. Standard key: in life, depends **on**, attitude **to/towards** life, hanker **after**, On the other hand, satisfied **with**, remain **in** anxiety, remain **in** great fear, live **in** incessant peace, afraid **of**.

### 2020 Q8 Transform
(a) Though I called him, he didn't respond. / Since I called him...
(b) What a nice painting it is!
(c) He tried hard but he could not reach his goal. / He tried hard and could not...
(d) His brother does not speak as correctly as he does.
(e) I was made to deliver a speech in the meeting (by him).
(f) He is trying so that he may survive well. / He is trying in a way that he may survive.
(g) Della sacrificed the dearest possession and proved her unfeigned love.

### 2021 Q4
a) I went to **the** bazaar with a view **to buying**...
b) Recently we **have been going** / **went** to Dhaka. (recently → present perfect)
c) My brother **returned** home last week. (last week → past simple)
d) That he bought the books **is** known to me. (That-clause singular)
e) You're right **to say so** → You're right **in saying so**? Actually "You're right to say so." is correct. Might intend "You are right to say so." Accept.
f) When **was** the book first published?
g) Dickens is a **popular** novelist. (populous = densely populated)

### 2021 Q5
a) I have never thought that I would go there. / Never have I thought...
b) To err is not inhuman? Better: No one is free from error. / Not to err is not human → It is not that to err is inhuman. Standard: Nobody is perfect.
c) The misty weather made us cancel our tour. / Because of misty weather...
d) No other scenery... is as charming as Kuakata? Actually St Martin is... → Kuakata is not so charming as St Martin.
e) The work was helped to be done? → I was helped to do the work (by him) / The work was done with his help.
f) You should take regular exercise. (imperative → assertive)
g) If you read more, you can learn more. / Read more and you can learn more.

### 2021 Q6 Verbs
(a) began (b) was organized (c) was inaugurated (d) emphasized (e) attached.

### 2021 Q7 Articles
(a) An idle (b) an active? Actually "An idle man and an active man..." (c) the story (d) a grasshopper (e) X lazy? "really lazy" → ×. Or "a lazy one".

### 2021 Q8 Indirect
He said that he could chop some wood that day. I told him that I had a boy coming from the orphanage. He said that he was the boy. I asked in surprise if he was small. He replied that size didn't matter for chopping wood.

### 2022 Q4 Prepositions
(a) in (b) to (c) for (d) from (e) into (f) of (g) down (h) of (i) over / throughout (j) to.

### 2022 Q5 Corrections
a) It is **I** who **am** guilty / It is she who is guilty but not I.
b) Do you know **who I am**? (embedded question)
c) The boy is **too weak to**? Actually "The boy is not strong enough to carry..." The given "too strong to carry" is logically wrong → correct to "strong enough".
d) Had I the wings of a bird, I would have **met** you.
e) It is high time we **checked** pollution. (high time + past subjunctive)
f) She **has been reading** in the same class for three years.
g) **No sooner had he gone** to college **than** the bell rang.

### 2023 Q2 Verbs
(a) is (b) being polluted (c) do (d) contaminates (e) burning (f) happens (g) are burnt (h) held (i) checked (subjunctive) (j) will suffer.

### 2023 Q3 Indirect
The teacher asked the students if they had prepared their lesson that day. The students replied respectfully in the negative. The teacher asked why they had not prepared their lesson. A student replied that he had been suffering from a headache the previous day and added that he would do his work the next day.

### 2023 Q4 Prepositions
a) for (b) among (c) to (d) on (e) in (f) of (g) onto / on (h) on (i) before / at? Actually "till 6 o'clock" vs "before 6" (j) beside? Priya's house is **besides/beside** mine → **next to / near / beside** mine. Often answer: **beside** mine.

### 2023 Q5 Transform
a) Without leaving now, you will get caught. / Leave now or you will get caught.
b) Though we were not sure..., we volunteered.
→ We were not sure..., yet we volunteered.
c) Though I looked..., I could not...
→ I looked... but...
d) You should start now to finish... → If you want to finish..., start now.
e) Being a nurse, Maria's job was... → Maria was a nurse, so...
f) As we were bored, my cousins and I went... → We were bored, so we went...

### 2023 Q6 Articles
a) the truth / a lie (speak the truth)
b) an hour (150 miles an hour)
c) × Bristol (city)
d) a one-taka note (one = /wʌn/)
e) a BA but an MA (B consonant, M vowel sound)
f) × breakfast (meals ×)
g) a terrible headache
h) the whole afternoon.
