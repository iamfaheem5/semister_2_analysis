### 2020 Q9 — Paragraph: Safety Measures of Minimizing Corona Virus Infections

Corona virus spreads through droplets and contact. Safety measures are vital. First, wash hands for 20 seconds with soap, use sanitizer, avoid touching face. Second, wear mask properly, maintain 3-feet social distance, avoid crowds. Third, keep rooms ventilated, disinfect surfaces, isolate if symptomatic. Vaccination, boosting immunity, and following WHO guidelines further reduce risk. Collective responsibility can minimize infection. (95 words)

### 2020 Q11 — Amplify: Look before you leap

"Look before you leap" means think before acting. A leap is irreversible; hasty decisions cause regret. A student who chooses a subject without aptitude suffers; an investor who invests without research loses. History shows impulsive wars bring ruin. My uncle hurriedly bought land without verification and faced litigation. Thus caution, inquiry, counselling are wise. This does not mean endless hesitation, but prudent foresight. As the proverb says, discretion is the better part of valour. (110 words)

### 2021 Q9 — Food Adulteration / Dengue

*Food Adulteration*: Adding harmful substances to food for profit. Vendors mix brick dust in chilli, formalin in fish. It causes cancer, kidney failure. Causes: greed, weak law, lack of awareness. Prevention: mobile courts, BSTI testing, consumer awareness, exemplary punishment. Safe food is a right.

*Dengue*: Viral fever by Aedes mosquito, symptoms high fever, joint pain. Prevention: clean stagnant water, use nets, early diagnosis.

### 2023 Q9 — Report: Cutting Trees

**Pabna, 5 Aug 2023**: Indiscriminate tree felling for housing/brick kilns shrinks forest cover below 10%. Temperature rises, dust storms increase, biodiversity lost. Experts warn of desertification.
