## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P3. [Easy] [4 marks]** — Write a paragraph (120 words) on "Food Adulteration" (2021/2023) — definition, causes, effects, prevention. Use topic sentence + 3 developments + concluding sentence.

**P9. [Hard] [6 marks]** — Write paragraphs (two options each, choose one): (a) Corona Virus safety measures (2020 Q9) vs Dengue Fever (2021 alternative) (b) 4th Industrial Revolution (2022 Q10) vs Role of Students in Social Reconstruction (2023 Q10) Each 150 words with data (4IR: IoT, AI, robotics; Corona: hand wash, mask, distance).

**P-New. [Medium] [5 marks]** — Write a paragraph (130 words) on "Bad Effects of Unplanned Urbanization" OR "Bad Effects of Poly Bags" — include causes (migration, cheap plastic), effects (drain block, soil), solution (recycling, jute), with cohesive linkers (Firstly, Moreover, Consequently).
