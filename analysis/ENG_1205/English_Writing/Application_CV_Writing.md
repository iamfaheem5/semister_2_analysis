## Previous-Year Questions in this Subtopic

### 2020 — ENG-1205 — Q10 — [7 marks] Write an application to the Principal/Head of your college/Institute for the arrangement of a debate competition.

*Formal application: To Principal, Subject, Sir, body (purpose: reasoning/public speaking, proposal: next month, auditorium, funding, judges), request, Yours faithfully.*

### 2021 — ENG-1205 — Q10 — [8 marks] Write an application to the Principal/Head of your college/institute for the arrangement of study tour.

*Formal application: theory with practice, destination (Sundarbans/Software Park), transport/accommodation/guide, educational benefit.*

### 2022 — ENG-1205 — Q7 — [8 marks] Write an application to the Managing Director of Rangs Electronics Bangladesh for the post of a Computer Engineer along with a complete CV.

*Cover letter + CV: Source of advertisement (Daily Star 10 May), degree (BSc CSE, DU affiliated), GPA, skills (C/Python/Digital Logic), enclosures, complete CV attachment.*

### 2023 — ENG-1205 — Q8 — [8 marks] Write a complete CV for the post of a Computer Engineer in a Company.

*Full CV only: Header, Objective, Education (BSc CSE), Skills (C, Java, Python), Projects (Line Follower, 3-bit Comparator), Achievements, References, contact.*

## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P1. [Easy] [5 marks]** — You are applying for the post of Computer Engineer at Rangs Electronics (2022 Q7). Write a covering application (formal letter) to the Managing Director. Mention source of advertisement, your degree (BSc CSE, 1st year 2nd semester, Dhaka University affiliated), and enclosures (CV, certificates).

**P7. [Hard] [7 marks]** — Write a complete CV (with cover letter) for Computer Engineer (2023 Q8). Include: Header, Objective, Education (BSc CSE), Skills (C, Python, Digital Logic), Projects, Achievements, References. Format as professional CV.

**P10(a). [Hard] [7 marks]** — Application comparison: Write both (a) Application to Principal for debate competition (2020 Q10) vs study tour (2021 Q10) — compare structure. Highlight common parts (To, Subject, Sir, Body) vs distinct purpose paragraph. Write both in exam time 20 min each.
