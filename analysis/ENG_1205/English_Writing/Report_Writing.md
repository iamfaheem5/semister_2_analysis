## Previous-Year Questions in this Subtopic

### 2021 — ENG-1205 — Q11 — [7 marks] Write a report on the unchanged price hike of the essential commodities.

*Newspaper report: By Staff Reporter, Dhaka, date, lead (what: 30–40% rise, where: kitchen market, who suffers: low-income), findings (retailer vs syndicate), relief (TCB open market), recommendation (monitoring, OMS, action against hoarders). 150–180 words.*

### 2023 — ENG-1205 — Q9 — [7 marks] Write a report on "Food Adulteration" or "Cutting down Trees is a threat to our existence"

*Two options:*
- *Food Adulteration — formalin, brick dust, cancer, BSTI, mobile courts*
- *Cutting down Trees — Pabna 5 Aug, forest cover <10%, temperature/dust/biodiversity, demand social forestry, UNO plant 10 per felling. 150–180 words, by-line, dateline.*

## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P5. [Medium] [6 marks]** — Write a report (150 words) on "Price Hike of Essential Commodities" (2021 Q11) or "Food Adulteration" (2023 Q9) as a newspaper reporter. Include By-line, date, place, lead paragraph, findings, quotes, recommendations.

**P10(b). [Hard] [7 marks]** — Report: "Cutting down Trees is a threat to our existence" (2023 alternative) with cause-effect and statistics (deforestation 8% yearly). Write as Staff Correspondent, Pabna, with quotes from UNO/experts and headline.

**P-New. [Easy] [4 marks]** — Write a report (120 words) on "Road Accident in your locality" OR "Prize Giving Ceremony at your college" — practice report structure (Title, Byline, Dateline, Lead, Body, Conclusion) for exam speed.
