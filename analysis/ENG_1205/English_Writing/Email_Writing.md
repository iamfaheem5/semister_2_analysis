## Previous-Year Questions in this Subtopic

### 2020 — ENG-1205 — Q12 — [7 marks] Compose an Email to your bosom friend to celebrate his/her birthday.

*Informal warm tone: To, Subject: Happy Birthday!, Greeting, wish, plan to celebrate, cake, closing.*

### 2022 — ENG-1205 — Q8 — [7 marks] Write an e-mail to your friend condoling on his/her father's death.

*Formal condolence tone: To, Subject: Deep condolence, shock, sympathy, pray for Jannah, offer support, closing.*

### 2023 — ENG-1205 — Q7 — [7 marks] Write an e-mail to your friend congratulating him on his achievement of a fully funded scholarship for higher study abroad.

*Congratulatory formal: To, Subject: Congratulations on Fully Funded Scholarship!, achievement (Erasmus/foreign), pride, invitation to share experience, best wishes, 120 words.*

> Note: Email replaced Application in 2022–23 trend; all require proper email header (To, Subject, Dear/Sir, Body, Closing).

## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P2. [Easy] [4 marks]** — Write a condolence email (2022 Q8) to a friend whose father died (7 marks condensed to 4). Follow email format: To, Subject, Greeting, Body (sympathy, support), Closing.

**P8. [Hard] [7 marks]** — Write an email to your bosom friend to celebrate his/her birthday (2020 Q12) AND an email congratulating for fully funded scholarship abroad (2023 Q7). Show difference in tone. Both in proper email format.

**P-New. [Medium] [5 marks]** — Write an email (120 words) to your friend inviting him to your sister's wedding OR thanking him for birthday gift. Contrast formal vs informal: use "Dear Arif" vs "Dear Sir" appropriately. Include To, Subject, date.
