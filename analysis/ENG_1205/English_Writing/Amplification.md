## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P6. [Medium] [6 marks]** — Amplify the idea "Look before you leap" (2020 Q11) in 150 words. Structure: proverb meaning, illustration (historical/personal), moral, concluding proverb. Contrast with "He who hesitates is lost".

**P-New1. [Easy] [4 marks]** — Amplify "Honesty is the best policy" OR "A stitch in time saves nine" in 120 words. Use definition + example (shopkeeper/bazaar) + moral.

**P-New2. [Hard] [6 marks]** — Amplify "Knowledge is power" OR "Time and tide wait for none" — include counter-proverb balance (Knowledge without wisdom is dangerous). Write 150 words with cohesive devices (Firstly, For instance, Therefore, In conclusion).
