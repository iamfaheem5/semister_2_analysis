## Part B — 10 Original Practice Questions (Exam-Realistic)

### [Easy]

**P1. [5 marks] [Easy]**
You are applying for the post of Computer Engineer at Rangs Electronics (2022 Q7). Write a covering application (formal letter) to the Managing Director. Mention source of advertisement, your degree (BSc CSE, 1st year 2nd semester, Dhaka University affiliated), and enclosures (CV, certificates).

**P2. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Write a condolence email (2022 Q8) to a friend whose father died (7 marks condensed to 4). Follow email format: To, Subject, Greeting, Body (sympathy, support), Closing.

**P3. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Write a paragraph (120 words) on "Food Adulteration" (2021/2023) — definition, causes, effects, prevention. Use topic sentence + 3 developments + concluding sentence.

### [Medium]

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Write a dialogue (150 words) between two friends on: (a) FIFA World Cup 2022 (2021 Q12) or (b) Strengthening Cyber Security (2022 Q9). Maintain natural turn-taking, 10 exchanges.

**P5. [6 marks] [Medium]**
Write a report (150 words) on "Price Hike of Essential Commodities" (2021 Q11) or "Food Adulteration" (2023 Q9) as a newspaper reporter. Include By-line, date, place, lead paragraph, findings, quotes, recommendations.

**P6. [6 marks] [Medium]**
Amplify the idea "Look before you leap" (2020 Q11) in 150 words. Structure: proverb meaning, illustration (historical/personal), moral, concluding proverb. Contrast with "He who hesitates is lost".

### [Hard]

**P7. [7 marks] [Hard]**
Write a complete CV (with cover letter) for Computer Engineer (2023 Q8). Include: Header, Objective, Education (BSc CSE), Skills (C, Python, Digital Logic), Projects, Achievements, References. Format as professional CV.

**P8. [7 marks] [Hard]**
Write an email to your bosom friend to celebrate his/her birthday (2020 Q12) AND an email congratulating for fully funded scholarship abroad (2023 Q7). Show difference in tone. Both in proper email format.

**P9. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Write paragraphs (two options each, choose one):
(a) Corona Virus safety measures (2020 Q9) vs Dengue Fever (2021 alternative)
(b) 4th Industrial Revolution (2022 Q10) vs Role of Students in Social Reconstruction (2023 Q10)
Each 150 words with data (4IR: IoT, AI, robotics; Corona: hand wash, mask, distance).

**P10. [7 marks] [Hard]**
Application + Report combo: (a) Application to Principal for debate competition (2020 Q10) vs study tour (2021 Q10) — compare structure. (b) Report on "Cutting down Trees is a threat to our existence" (2023 alternative) with cause-effect and statistics (deforestation 8% yearly). Write both in exam time 45 min.

---

**How to use:** Practice with strict word limits: Email 120 words, Paragraph 150 words, Report 180 words, Dialogue 150 words, Application 150 words, CV 1 page. Time: 7 marks ≈ 20 min.
