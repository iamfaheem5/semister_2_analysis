## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P9. [Hard] [6 marks]** — Précis writing — Write a précis of the 2022 internationalization passage (both paragraphs) in 90–100 words. Must reflect both benefits and challenges, and the concluding nuanced approach. Give title.
