## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P1. [Easy] [4 marks]** — Correct any four: (a) I am not in the habit of taking heavy lunch. (b) He is looking forward to meet you. (c) The costs of daily necessaries is high. (d) No sooner had he reached the station, the train left. (e) She has been reading for three years. (f) Had I been there, I would help you.

**P8. [Hard] [5 marks]** — Correct the errors and explain rule: (a) That he bought the books are known to me. (subject-verb agreement) (b) It is she who am guilty. (pronoun + verb) (c) No sooner he had gone to college, the bell rang. (inversion) (d) It is high time we should check pollution. (subjunctive) (e) Do you know who am I? (embedded question word order)

**P-New. [Medium] [5 marks]** — Correct any five from mixed PYQ bank: (a) I'm not in the habit to take... (b) I can't but to laugh... (c) I went to bazaar with a view to buy... (d) The boy is too strong to carry... (e) It is high time we should check... (f) A man is known by the company he is kept. Justify gerund/infinitive rule for each.
