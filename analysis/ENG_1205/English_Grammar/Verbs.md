## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P2. [Easy] [3 marks]** — Fill verbs: Air (a)___(be) polluted, Vehicles (b)___(emit) smoke, It is high time we (c)___(take) steps. (Choose: is, emit, took) — *Distinguishes present, participle, and high time + past subjunctive.*

**P-New2. [Medium] [5 marks]** — Correct form (Parents pattern): Parents (a)___(be) blessings, When child (b)___(bear), joys (c)___(know) no bounds, They (d)___(start) (e)___(think) of (f)___(nurse). Explain gerund vs infinitive after start/think.

**P-New3. [Hard] [5 marks]** — Mixed verbs: Trees (a)___(contribute), fair (b)___(begin) recently, It (c)___(organize) by admin, It (d)___(inaugurate) yesterday, speakers (e)___(emphasize). Include passive voice logic (was organized/inaugurated).
