## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P5. [Medium] [5 marks]** — Transform: (a) Though he is poor, he is honest. (Compound) (b) The weather was stormy but we went hiking. (Complex) (c) He is too weak to walk. (Use so...that) (d) She is the best student. (Positive degree) (e) He said, "I will do it tomorrow." (Indirect)

**P10. [Hard] [6 marks]** — Comprehensive transformation: Convert correctly: (a) If you don't leave now, you will miss the train. (Simple with "Unless/Without") (b) To err is human. (Negative without changing meaning) (c) Sacrificing the dearest possession, Della proved her love. (Compound with "and") (d) He made me do the work. (Passive with "I was made...") (e) Reading more, you can learn more. (Compound with "If you read...")

**P-New. [Easy] [4 marks]** — Transform any four (simple↔complex↔compound): (a) Though he tried hard, he failed (Compound) (b) I called him but he didn't respond (Complex) (c) Despite rain, we went (Compound) (d) He is more clever than I (Positive). Identify conjunction mapping (Though → but/and, Despite → though).
