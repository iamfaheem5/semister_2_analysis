**P3. [3 marks] [Easy]**

### [Medium]

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Fill prepositions: (a) hanker ___ money, (b) depend ___ something, (c) grateful ___ someone, (d) divide ___ (among/between) two vs many, (e) look ___ something, (f) fond ___ , (g) indebted ___, (h) essential ___.

**P5. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Transform:
(a) Though he is poor, he is honest. (Compound)
(b) The weather was stormy but we went hiking. (Complex)
(c) He is too weak to walk. (Use so...that)
(d) She is the best student. (Positive degree)
(e) He said, "I will do it tomorrow." (Indirect)

**P6. [5 marks] [Medium]**

### [Hard]

**P7. [5 marks] [Hard]**
Article with cross (×): Explain when to use ×:
(a) ___ man is mortal (abstract)
(b) ___ honesty is the best policy
(c) Go to ___ school vs go to ___ hospital
(d) ___ Sun rises in ___ east
(e) He is ___ B.A. but she is ___ M.A.
(f) Eat ___ breakfast vs eat ___ dinner

**P8. [5 marks] [Hard]**
Correct the errors and explain rule:
(a) That he bought the books are known to me. (subject-verb agreement)
(b) It is she who am guilty. (pronoun + verb)
(c) No sooner he had gone to college, the bell rang. (inversion)
(d) It is high time we should check pollution. (subjunctive)
(e) Do you know who am I? (embedded question word order)

**P9. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Preposition + phrasal: Distinguish (a) look for vs look after vs look for? (b) hanker after vs hanker for? (c) grateful to vs grateful for? (d) different from vs different to? Use each in sentence as in exams (2020/2022).

**P10. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Comprehensive transformation: Convert correctly:
(a) If you don't leave now, you will miss the train. (Simple with "Unless/Without")
(b) To err is human. (Negative without changing meaning)
(c) Sacrificing the dearest possession, Della proved her love. (Compound with "and")
(d) He made me do the work. (Passive with "I was made...")
(e) Reading more, you can learn more. (Compound with "If you read...")

---

**How to use:** Practice articles/prepositions with × logic, verb sequence (have/had, high time + past), transformation keyword mapping (Though→In spite of/But, Complex↔Simple via participle/infinitive).
