## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P5. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Use Euler's method with $$h=0.1$$ for $$\frac{dy}{dx}=x+y^2,\;y(0)=1$$ to find $$y(0.4)$$ (four steps). Show table $$x_n, y_n, f_n$$: $$y_{n+1}=y_n+0.1(x_n+y_n^2)$$.

**P6. [6 marks] [Medium]**
Use Euler's method to find $$y(0.1)$$ for $$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y-x}{y+x},\;y(0)=1$$ (single step $$h=0.1$$). Explain why this ODE is not Lipschitz at $$(0,0)$$ but is okay at $$(0,1)$$. Compute $$y_1=1+0.1\cdot1=1.1$$? Check.

**P-new. [4 marks] [Easy]**
State Euler's method $$y_{n+1}=y_n+hf(x_n,y_n)$$ and do one step for $$\frac{dy}{dx}=x+y^2,\ y(0)=1,\ h=0.1$$ to get $$y(0.1)$$. Compare with Taylor method result.
