## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P9. [7 marks] [Hard]**
Find $$y(2.2)$$ using Euler's method for $$\frac{dy}{dx}=-x y^2,\;y(2)=1,\;h=0.05$$ (four steps: $$2.0\to2.05\to2.10\to2.15\to2.20$$). Compare with exact solution $$1/y = x^2/2 -1$$, exact $$y(2.2)=0.704$$. Discuss error reduction if $$h=0.025$$.

**P10. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Replicate 2020 Q2(c): $$y'=0.1\sqrt y+0.4x^2,\;y(2)=4$$. Perform Euler with $$h=0.1$$ for 5 steps to $$y(2.5)$$ and with $$h=0.05$$ for 10 steps. Show that the $$h=0.05$$ result is closer to the (unknown) exact solution and estimate Richardson extrapolation.

**P-new. [5 marks] [Medium]**
For $$y'= -x y^2,\ y(2)=1$$, compute Euler with $$h=0.1$$ (2 steps to 2.2) vs $$h=0.05$$ (4 steps). Compare errors vs exact $$2/(x^2-2)$$; demonstrate $$O(h)$$ global error halving when step halved.
