## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P2. [3 marks] [Easy]**
Evaluate $$\displaystyle \int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x}}$$ and $$\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\infty} x e^{-x^2}dx$$ . Determine convergence/divergence with full improper-limit reasoning.

**P-new. [4 marks] [Medium]**
Evaluate $$\displaystyle \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x}{x^4+1}dx$$ using even/odd symmetry and substitution $$u=x^2$$. Show convergence and compute value $$\pi/(2\sqrt{2})$$? Verify via contour or Beta.

**P-new2. [5 marks] [Hard]**
Discuss convergence of: (i) $$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x}{x^2+1}dx$$ vs $$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{x}{x^4+1}dx$$, (ii) $$\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x}}$$ vs $$\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{dx}{x^{3/2}}$$. State p-test for improper integrals $$\int_{1}^{\infty} x^{-p} dx$$.
