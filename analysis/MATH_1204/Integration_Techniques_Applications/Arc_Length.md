## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P7-part. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Show that length of one arch of the cycloid $$x=r(\theta-\sin\theta),\,y=r(1-\cos\theta),\ 0\le\theta\le2\pi$$ is $$8r$$. Derive $$L=\int_0^{2\pi} r\sqrt{(1-\cos\theta)^2+\sin^2\theta}\,d\theta$$.

**P-new. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Find perimeter of cardioid $$r=2(1-\cos\theta)$$: show $$L=16$$. Compare with $$y=\ln\sec x$$ from $$0$$ to $$\pi/3$$ giving $$\ln(2+\sqrt3)$$ — verify both via direct integration.

**P-new2. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Use polar formula to show arc length of $$r(1+\cos\theta)=2$$ from $$0$$ to $$\pi/2$$ is $$\sqrt2+\ln(1+\sqrt2)$$. Hint: $$r=2/(1+\cos\theta)$$, compute $$r'$$.
