## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P4. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Find the surface area generated by revolving the parabola $$y^2=4ax$$ from $$x=0$$ to $$x=a$$ about the x-axis. Show that $$S = \frac{8\pi a^2}{3}(2\sqrt2-1)$$ .

**P7-part. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Also find the surface area generated by revolving the cycloid $$x=a(\theta+\sin\theta), y=a(1+\cos\theta)$$ about its base (x-axis), and verify it equals $$\frac{64\pi a^2}{3}$$.

**P8(b). [4 marks] [Hard]**
The ellipse $$\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1$$ (i.e. $$x^2+4y^2=16$$) is rotated about its minor axis (y-axis). Derive and evaluate the surface area of the oblate spheroid: $$S = 2\pi a^2\left(1+\frac{1-e^2}{e}\tanh^{-1}e\right)$$ with $$a=4, b=2$$ and compute numeric value.
