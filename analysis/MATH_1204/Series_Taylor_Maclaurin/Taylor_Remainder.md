## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P1-part. [3 marks] [Easy]**
State Taylor's theorem with Lagrange remainder. Write condition for infinite Taylor series convergence: $$R_n\to0$$.

**P-new. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Prove Taylor's theorem with Lagrange remainder: $$f(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)+\cdots+\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n+R_n$$. Outline mean-value / induction proof.

**P-new2. [5 marks] [Hard]**
Give remainder estimate for $$f(x)=\cos x$$ at $$a=0$$ using $$|R_n|\le |x|^{n+1}/(n+1)!$$. How many terms needed to approximate $$\cos(0.1)$$ to $$10^{-6}$$ accuracy?
