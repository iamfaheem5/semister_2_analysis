## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P3. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Find the quadratic (second-degree) Taylor polynomial of $$f(x)=\cos x$$ at $$a=0$$. Use it to approximate $$\cos(0.1)$$ and bound the error.

**P1-part. [3 marks] [Easy]**
Write Maclaurin series for $$e^x$$, $$\log(1+x)$$ and $$e^{\sin x}$$ up to $$x^4$$ term. Memorize: $$\log(1+x)=x-x^2/2+x^3/3-\cdots$$.

**P7-part. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Expand $$e^{\sin x}$$ up to $$x^4$$ and $$\log(1+x)$$ up to $$x^5$$. Use them to find $$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^{\sin x}-1-x-x^2/2}{x^3}$$.
