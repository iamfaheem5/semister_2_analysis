## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P4-part. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Find first three terms of Taylor series for $$\ln x$$ at $$x=1$$ and at $$x=2$$. Verify: $$\ln x = (x-1)-(x-1)^2/2+(x-1)^3/3-\cdots$$ and $$\ln x = \ln2 + (x-2)/2 -(x-2)^2/8+\cdots$$.

**P7. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Expand $$e^{\sin x}$$ up to $$x^4$$: $$e^{\sin x}=1+x+x^2/2 -x^4/8+\cdots$$. Show steps via composition: $$\sin x = x -x^3/6+\cdots$$, then $$e^{u}=1+u+u^2/2+u^3/6+\cdots$$.

**P4-full. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Expand $$2x^3+7x^2+x-6$$ in powers of $$(x-2)$$. Verify by expanding result to recover original polynomial. Also give nth Taylor polynomial for $$\ln x$$ at $$x=2$$.
