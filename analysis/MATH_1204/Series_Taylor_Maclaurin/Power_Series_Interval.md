Show that the series $$\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{4.7.10....(3n+1)}{1.2.3......n}x^n$$ converges if $$x < \frac13$$ and diverges if $$x \ge \frac13$$.

## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P6. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Show that $$\sum \frac{4\cdot7\cdot10\cdots(3n+1)}{1\cdot2\cdot3\cdots n}x^n$$ converges for $$|x|<1/3$$ and diverges for $$|x|\ge1/3$$ (ratio test). State radius $$R=1/3$$ and interval $$(-1/3,1/3)$$, discuss endpoints.

**P4-part. [5 marks] [Medium]**
Expand $$2x^3+7x^2+x-6$$ in powers of $$(x-2)$$ via Taylor: $$f(x)=f(2)+f'(2)(x-2)+f''(2)/2!(x-2)^2+f'''(2)/6(x-2)^3$$. Verify.

**P-new. [4 marks] [Hard]**
Find interval of convergence for $$\sum \left(\frac{2n+3}{3n+2}\right)^n x^n$$? Use root test: $$\limsup |a_n|^{1/n}=2/3$$, so $$R=3/2$$. Discuss.
