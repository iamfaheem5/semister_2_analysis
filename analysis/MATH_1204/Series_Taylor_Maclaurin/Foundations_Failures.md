## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P2. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Prove that if $$\sum a_n$$ converges then $$a_n\to0$$. Give a counterexample where $$a_n\to0$$ but $$\sum a_n$$ diverges (harmonic series). Test $$\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{n^2}{5n^2+4}$$ for convergence using this lemma (diverges since $$a_n\not\to0$$).

**P8. [6 marks] [Hard]**
Discuss failures of Taylor series: (a) function not infinitely differentiable at point (e.g. $$|x|$$ at 0), (b) series converges but not to function (e.g. $$e^{-1/x^2}$$ at 0), (c) radius of convergence limited by complex singularities (e.g. $$\log(1+x)$$ at $$x=-1$$). Give one example for each.

**P-new. [3 marks] [Medium]**
Define convergence/divergence of series via partial sums $$S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$$. Show $$\sum 1/(\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt n)$$ diverges by telescoping comparison.
