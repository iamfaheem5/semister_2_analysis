## Practice Questions (Subtopic-specific)

**P10(b). [5 marks] [Medium]**
Solve IVP $$y'+2xy =f(x)$$, $$y(0)=2$$, $$f=x$$ for $$0\le x<1$$ else 0, using integrating factor $$e^{x^2}$$ piecewise and continuity at $$x=1$$. Show $$y(1^-)=y(1^+)$$.

**P-new. [4 marks] [Easy]**
Verify that $$y=(x^2+c)e^{-x}$$ solves $$y'+y=2xe^{-x}$$. Find $$c$$ for $$y(-1)=e+3$$. Also solve $$y'+y=x,\ y(0)=4$$ explicitly: $$y= x-1+5e^{-x}$$.

**P-new2. [5 marks] [Hard]**
Solve IVP $$y'+y=x,\ y(0)=4$$ and discuss long-term behaviour. Compare with numerical Euler estimate for same IVP with $$h=0.1$$ — what is truncation error at $$x=0.4$$?
